Place

—

You can point to anywhere in the world with a Google Earth, Google Maps or OpenStreetMap link, latitude/longitude (decimal or degrees-minutes-seconds), a Plus Code, or a KML/KMZ/GeoJSON file (📂 or drag it onto the page). With a Google Earth (web) link, the viewing direction is reproduced too.

Click the map or the ground in the 3D view to drop a pin there. “Move camera here” or the F key moves the camera to the pin, keeping its height and direction. “Stand here” puts you at the pin at eye level. The arrows move to the neighboring area.

What to build

Building heights come from the map data (height or levels) when present; otherwise they are guessed from the use and the size. Guessed heights change with “🎲 Re-roll unknown heights” below.

Export

Save the picture or model with the toolbar under the viewer (PNG, JPEG, WebP, SVG, GLB, FBX, OBJ). The options below apply to those files.

You may use the pictures you make, commercially or not. When you publish them, credit the map data as “© OpenStreetMap contributors” near the picture or in the credits of your work. If you share the 3D models, include the same credit (the map data is under the ODbL).

About the map data

Buildings, roads, rivers and so on come from OpenStreetMap, the free map made by everyone, under the ODbL. Map data is fetched from the Overpass API; place search uses Nominatim; elevation comes from Terrain Tiles (AWS Open Data). Downloaded data is remembered in this browser for 7 days.
